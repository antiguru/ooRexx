count 18
/* A number's displayed form is fixed when the value is created, not when it
   is displayed. Both DIGITS and FORM are captured at creation:

   y is computed under DIGITS 9 and keeps 9 significant digits even after
   DIGITS drops to 3. x is computed under FORM ENGINEERING and keeps its
   engineering-notation spelling ("10E+9") even after FORM switches to
   SCIENTIFIC -- switching FORM again does not re-render an existing value,
   it only affects values created afterwards. */
numeric digits 9
y = 1 / 3
numeric digits 3
say y

numeric form engineering
x = 1e10 + 0
say x
numeric form scientific
say x
