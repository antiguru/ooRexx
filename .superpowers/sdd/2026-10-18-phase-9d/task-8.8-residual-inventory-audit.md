## Guarded trace destination

The authorized conditional deferral was exercised as a reported Phase 10 handoff, while the table and queued header remain unreconciled. [The queued ruling](../queued/2026-10-19-guarded-trace-destination-spin.md:1) says 9d if lifting the pinned-guard-wait limit is tractable, else Phase 10, and [the batch dispatch](task-8-batches.md:24) explicitly permits reporting a proposed Phase 10 owner instead of a fix. [Task 8.1's report](task-8.1-report.md:105) determines that the lift is not tractable in that batch and names Phase 10 continuation activities as owner. [The approved review](task-8.1-review.md:24) reproduces the open hang, calls the report compliant, and [controller progress](progress.md:173) records the proposed Phase 10 owner immediately before accepting Task 8.1 as complete.

The two symptoms remain distinct and neither is fixed by this metadata reconciliation. In the retained [guarded-spin records](../../../docs/superpowers/records/2026-10-18-phase-9d/t8.1/probes/guarded-spin/spin.oracle.out), `spin.rex` has oracle stdout `done 1`, empty stderr and rc 0, while head has empty stdout, a wrapper kill diagnostic on stderr and recorded rc 137 after the six-second kill described in the report. For `m2.rex`, oracle stdout is `lines 10 results A B`, empty stderr and rc 0, while head stdout is empty and stderr is the pinned-guard refusal with rc 120, in the adjacent per-side records. [The original re-review](task-6.10-rereview.md:111) calls the spin a regression against `d2e55befa`, whereas [the round-3 re-review](task-6.10-rereview-r3.md:87) says the two-activity blocking shape was a pre-existing delivery failure made loud, with the parent printing only five lines.

Frozen source corroborates why a local yield change is insufficient. At `1d8b03220`, `environment/route.rs:228` delivers a line inside `pinned!(..., PinKind::TraceWrapper, ...)`, and `scheduler.rs:2381-2415` explains and enforces suppression of a pinned yield while a destination activation holds a guard. Letting that nested call yield can place a second guard waiter above the activity that must release the guard, matching `lib.rs:1020`'s inverted-wait refusal. [Deviation 14](../../../docs/superpowers/plans/phase-4-exclusions.txt:1493) already licenses that refusal with `OWNER: none`, but does not claim to fix the spinning destination or replace its Phase 10 handoff.

The nonlocal work is making trace emission a resumable driver point at every emitting site, as [Task 8.1 explicitly records](task-8.1-report.md:111). This changes the nested execution boundary that currently keeps Rust frames pinned rather than merely changing a guard predicate or timeout. The evidence supports recording the conditional outcome as `phase:10`, with the queued item still OPEN and owned by Phase 10 continuation-activities work. No numbered Phase 10 task or individual assignee appears in these records, so assigning either would require controller planning rather than inventing provenance.
