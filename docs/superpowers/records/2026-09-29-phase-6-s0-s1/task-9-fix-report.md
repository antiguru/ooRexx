`Object~new` uses. `begin_init(interp, object, args)` (`dispatch/class_protocol.rs`) is that half,
factored out of `native_new`: `begin_send(object, INIT, ..)`, answering `object` directly on `Ran` or
through `Then::Answer(object)` once `finish_send` reaches the tail. The `NEW` rows of `Array`,
`StringTable`, `Directory`, `Bag`, `IdentityTable`, `Relation`, `Set`, `Table`, `Class`, `Message`,
`WeakReference`, `String`, `Stem`, `MutableBuffer` (`RESUMABLE_CLASS_METHODS`) and `Package`
(`package::RESUMABLE_CLASS_METHODS`) are now `NativeBegin`s ending in `begin_init`;
`native_directory_new`'s subclass arm no longer goes through `complete_native`. The `OF`
factories (`Array`, `Bag`, `Set`, `Queue`, `List`) still send `INIT` synchronously and pin as
