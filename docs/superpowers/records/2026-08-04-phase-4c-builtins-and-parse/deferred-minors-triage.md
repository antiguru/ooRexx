  Found: the RSS discrepancy at `:1270-1273` (already inside the Task 13 block), a crash-filing decision at `:1135-1144` (not a project defect, a filing decision left to the user), and a `## Deferred decision: splitting run.rs` section at `:1158` (an architectural deferral with its own destination already recorded, not a minor finding).

**The critical thing the count-by-phrase search misses, which a plain grep on `minor (deferred):` also misses:** three of the eight already-triaged lines are the *first sentence of a paragraph bundling several sub-findings*, and the final review's triage table addressed only that first sentence.

`:784` (Task 8) has three; only the first (`ProgramInput::Bytes`) was triaged.
`:905` (Task 9) has one, so nothing was missed there.
So the exact-phrase search itself under-counts even where it fires.

**Total individual findings across all twelve blocks: 37**, not the "roughly 33" the final review estimated (its number came from counting blocks and headings loosely, mine from opening every paragraph and counting sub-items).
37 minus the 8 already ruled on (all 8 are single sub-items, three of them the head of a multi-item paragraph) leaves **29 to triage here**.
