# Items 3 and 5: one fusion landed, one sized and declined, 2026-09-21

BASE is `bc26d7936`. One commit: **`725aa8863`**, "Branch from the condition op
itself, so a test and its jump are one dispatch". Item 5 was sized on the pinned
axis and **not built**.

All instruction figures are `valgrind --tool=callgrind`'s `summary:` line, two
rounds per build, each build in its own `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`, with each binary's
`sha256` beside its figures. All op figures are the summed execution counts at
the `jmp *` addresses inside every `Interp::run_ops_from` instantiation, the
method `2026-09-20-instructions-per-op.md` sets out.

## What landed

`Op::Condition` and `Op::JumpUnless` become one `Op::ConditionJump`. The pair is
emitted together at both sites that emit either -- an `IF` whose condition
`native_shape` accepts, and a listed `WHEN`'s -- so it is statically adjacent in
every stream there is.

**The fusion is made where the ops are emitted, not by a pass over a finished
stream.** That is what dissolves the renumbering hazard; the enumeration below
is what showed it was the design to choose.

`Op::JumpUnless` stays, for the two arrivals that are not a validated condition:
an `IF` whose condition falls to `Op::EvalExpr`, and an `Op::WhenTest`.

The fused op still validates, still raises 34.1 for an `IF` and 34.2 for a
`WHEN`, and still emits the `>>>` line. Only the write-back to the register is
gone, because the branch was its only reader.

## "Ops removed x 20" is a floor, not a ceiling

The queued item derived **~0.56%** from 5,900,006 ops at 20.0 instructions each.
The measured figure is **-1.6747%**, and the measured price of a removed op here
is **54.8 instructions**.

20.0 Ir is the price of a dispatch that *does nothing*, derived from deleting
trace ops. This fusion removes more than a dispatch. The split is **34.8 of the
54.8** in register traffic, and it is:

* on the write side, `ObjRef::small_int(i64::from(holds))` and
  `self.roots.set_temp(registers, reg, logical)`;
* on the read side, `self.roots.temp_at(registers, reg)` and
  `register_holds`'s two constant comparisons, its `decode()` and the match on
  it.

None of that survives, because the branch was the write-back's only reader.

**So a ceiling derived as "ops removed times 20" understates every candidate of
this shape, and by how much depends on how much register traffic sat between the
pair.** Anything remaining on the queued list that was sized that way is sized
low.

### Why the hot ones are not adjacent

Ten further `Store` ops are constant-fed with exactly one op in between, and
that op is `Op::TraceLiteral`:

    110: Clause index=26 end=114   ; 45: key1='Key Bee'
    111: Const dst=5 konst=7
    112: TraceLiteral src=5
    113: Store index=26 at=13 src=5

The ten: `flag=0` and `p0='b'` (line 42), `key1='Key Bee'` (45), `flag=0` (66),
`flag=1` (68), `flag=1` (73), and the four `rc='...'` clauses (78-81) -- the hot
half of the 1000-clause body.

Those echoes exist because `trace_flow::analyse` answers `Unknown` for the whole
body: two of the three `TRACE` instructions are `trace value <expr>`, which is
bottom to any static analysis. **Item 5's value on `rexxcps` is a function of
item 2, not of anything about the fusion.**

**The hot-body route is 1,740,000 ops per run. That figure is measured**, by a
parallel investigation, and it supersedes a 2,000,000 I derived by hand from the
loop structure. The ~260,000 gap is most likely my counting line 66's
`when flag==0 then flag=0` as taken when it is not -- one 14-per-pass site at
20,000 passes. The conclusion does not move either way.

### The threshold was computed at the wrong price, and the error is larger than one factor

Two corrections to the 0.165% that item 5 was declined against, in opposite
directions to each other but both in item 5's favour:

* **54.8 Ir is not transferable to item 5.** It decomposes as ~20 of dispatch
  plus ~34.8 of the handoff *this* pair had. A constant-load-and-store fusion
  removes `set_temp` and `temp_at` and **none of the rest** -- a strict subset,
  the cheaper half. So 54.8 is an upper bound for item 5, 20.0 is the lower
  bound, and nothing between them has been measured.
* **A three-op fusion removes two ops per execution, not one.** The hot pairs are
  separated by a live `TraceLiteral`, so reaching them means fusing
  `LoadConstant` + `TraceLiteral` + `Store` into one op. At 1,740,000 executions
  that is ~3,480,000 ops, not 1,740,000.

| price | ops removed | instructions | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20.0 Ir, the floor | 3,480,000 | 69.6M | **0.33%** |
| 54.8 Ir, the ceiling | 3,480,000 | 190.7M | **0.90%** |

**Even the floor clears 0.3%.** All of this is DERIVED, and rests on the
measured 1,740,000; that number is the one to re-derive before anyone acts on
it.

### Is the three-op fusion coherent?

Yes, and structurally it is the same shape as what was built, from what was read
in the code:

* The three ops are pushed adjacently in one place. `compile.rs`'s `Assignment`
  arm calls `push_value`, whose `ExprKind::Literal` arm pushes
  `Op::Const { dst, konst }` and then, `if echoes_values`,
  `Op::TraceLiteral { src: dst }`; the arm then pushes `Op::Store`. So the
  three-op fusion is emittable at the same site by the same emission-time
  technique, with the same "nothing renumbers" property.
* Nothing is skipped. The driver's `Op::TraceLiteral` arm gates on the setting
  and calls `echo_literal`; a fused op does the same gate and the same echo,
  then the store.
* **It removes more register traffic than the two-op version, not less.**
  `TraceLiteral` is a *second* reader of the register, so fusing all three lets
  the value live in a local: one `set_temp` and **two** `temp_at`s go, against
  one and one. Its per-op price should therefore sit nearer 54.8 than a plain
  `Const`+`Store` fusion's would.
* Width is fine: `{ index: u32, at: PlanSlot, konst: u32 }` is 12 bytes, inside
  the `size_of::<Op>() == 16` assert, and the `src` field the echo needed
  disappears because the fused op holds the value directly.
* GC is fine, and this was checked rather than assumed: `Interp::interned_literal`
  (`value.rs:117`) takes `alloc_immortal_with`, and `lib.rs:5456` records that a
  chunk's interned literals are immortal. So the constant needs no register to
  root it, which is what makes dropping the register write safe.

**Not looked at:** `echo_literal` itself, only the driver arm's gate and comment.
Whether the echo path can raise or re-enter in a way the current op ordering
depends on is unchecked, and is the thing to check before building it.

**Two things for the decision, not for the implementer:**

1. **Item 2 may obsolete it.** Those `TraceLiteral` ops exist only because the
   body is `Unknown`. If item 2 lands they leave the hot body, the ten sites
   become strictly adjacent, and the plain two-op fusion covers them -- at which
   point a three-op variant is dead weight carrying its own arms.
2. **It is four new arms, not one.** `Const`/`LoadConstant` x echoed/unechoed.
   The controls here show one arm *removed* cost nothing; they do not license
   four *added*. That needs its own `emptyloop`/`varlookup` control run.

## What is keyed by an op index, and how it was checked

The enumeration was done **first**, before any of the fusion was written, and the
emission-site design was chosen because of what it showed.

Method: on the clean tree, with `crates/rexx-exec/src` copied to the scratchpad
first and restored from that copy afterwards (no git involved, so no risk to
uncommitted work), `Op::Clause`'s `end`, `Op::Jump`'s and `Op::JumpUnless`'s
`target`, and `Chunk::op_of`'s element type were changed from `u32` to a newtype
`OpIx(u32)` carrying no arithmetic and no `From`.
`cargo build --workspace --all-targets` was then run repeatedly, fixing each
error and re-running, **until the error list was empty**. Every site below is one
rustc named; because the list was driven to empty, the enumeration is complete
rather than sampled.

Production:

* `Op::Clause { end }` -- `ir.rs:72`. Written by `compile::close_region`; read by
  the driver to bound the region slice (`chunk.ops_in(pc + 1, end)`), by
  `assert_exec_regions_hold_nothing_else` and
  `assert_region_ops_name_their_clause`, and as the region's fallthrough `next`.
* `Op::Jump { target }` -- `ir.rs:258`.
* `Op::JumpUnless { target }` -- `ir.rs:261`; now also `Op::ConditionJump { target }`.
* `Chunk::op_of: Vec<u32>` -- `ir.rs:564`, instruction index to op index, read
  through `Chunk::op_at`.
* `compile::first_op_of` -- the same map without the `Before` ops in front,
  which `PatchKind::Enter` resolves against.
* `compile::Patch::op` -- the op a forward jump rewrites.
* `compile::op_index` -- the sole producer of all of the above.
* `drive.rs:55` and `drive.rs:65` -- the two frame structs' `op_end`, built by
  `drive::op_at` from `targets.body_end` and `otherwise_end`, with `u32::MAX` as
  the sentinel at `:94` and `:2180`.
* `drive::Settled::At`, `drive::RegionEnd::At`, `drive::BranchEnd::At`.
* `Interp::run_ops` and `run_ops_from`'s `at` parameter, and `pc`, `stop` and
  `next` inside it.
* `drive::op_at`, the helper.
* `run.rs:343` `FlatLoop::op_body`, `run.rs:6273` `flat_loop_start`'s `op_body`
  parameter, `run.rs:441` `FlatStep::Body`.

Tests: the hand-built op streams in `compile.rs`'s own unit tests, four
`chunk.op_of` assertions in `golden_tests.rs`, one `end as usize` in
`corpus_shape_tests.rs`.

**Named by the brief and *not* keyed by an op index.** rustc flagged none of them
when the index type changed, and each was then read to confirm it is dense over
its own sites in emission order: `Chunk::hints` (keyed by `Op::Arith`'s `hint`,
handed out by `Hints::reserve`), `Chunk::calls` (keyed by `site`,
`Calls::reserve`), `Chunk::consts` and `Chunk::interned` (keyed by `konst`),
`Chunk::interned_symbols` (keyed by `SymbolId`), `Chunk::positions` and
`Chunk::settings` (instruction index), and every op's `index` and `slot` field,
and `PlanSlot`. The brief's specific worry -- that the hint tables are "dense
over sites rather than over the stream" -- is real but orthogonal: they renumber
if an `Arith` or a call op is *deleted*, and neither item deletes one.

**Why the hazard does not arise in what was built.** `op_of`, `first_op_of`, the
patch list and each `Clause`'s `end` are all taken from `op_index(&ops)` during
the same forward walk that emits the fused op, so every one of them is
consistent with the shorter stream by construction. A peephole pass over a
finished stream would have had to remap all fourteen production entries above;
the emission-site fusion remaps none.

## Hazards the brief named, against what was built

* **The register must be dead after the fused op.** It is. `ConditionJump` no
  longer writes the logical back, and the `JumpUnless` that read it is gone.
  `reg` is the `IF`'s or `WHEN`'s own register, allocated at the top of its
  `compile` arm and released at the end of it; the branch was its only reader. It
  keeps the unvalidated value it came in with, which is a live handle rather than
  a hole, so nothing about rooting changes.
* **Nothing may branch between the two ops.** Nothing can: a jump target is
  `first_op_of[i]` or `op_of[i]`, both of which name an instruction's own first
  op, and the fused op sits inside a clause region rather than at one of those.
* **No per-clause conditional was added**, which is the failure item 2 hit. The
  driver has one arm where it had two and one jump-table entry where it had two.
  The controls above are the evidence.

## Concerns

1. **`2026-09-20-instructions-per-op.md`'s op-mix table is a `<true>`-only table,
   and other items are being sized from it.** Its `Condition` 5,900,006 and
   `JumpUnless` 5,900,006 rows omit the `<false>` instantiation, which carries
   another 560,000 of each -- measured here, 8,400,000 -> 7,840,000. Every
   executed-count row in that table is understated by whatever its `<false>`
   share is, and nothing in the document says so. It is the denominator for
   items 4 through 6.
2. **The 20 Ir constant is a floor and the queued list is sized in it.** Item 3
   was sized 0.56% and measured 1.67%.
3. **`Op::TraceFunction` is emitted unconditionally, and that looks like a gap in
   item 1.** In `push_native`'s call arm, `TraceLiteral`, `TraceOperator`,
   `TracePrefix` and `TraceArgument` are each behind `if echoes_values`;
   `Op::TraceFunction` is pushed at both of its sites with no such gate, and the
   driver gates it at *run time* instead
   (`if !self.tracing_intermediates() { continue; }`). So a clause the analysis
   settled as `Known` and non-echoing still carries a `TraceFunction` dispatch
   per call that does nothing but test and continue. `rexxcps` has 8 statically,
   but its body is `Unknown` throughout so gating it would change nothing there
   -- the gain is on exactly the programs item 1 was built for. **Its executed
   cost was not measured** and this was not pursued: it is item 1's territory, it
   looks like a one-line emission gate, and it is the only value-echo op item 1
   does not reach.
4. **`Op::JumpUnless` now has two live producers and no benchmark emits either**
   -- an `IF` outside `native_shape`, and a listed `WHEN` falling to
   `Op::WhenTest`. 24 of them across the 621-program census. That arm is
   exercised by the corpus and the golden tests only.
